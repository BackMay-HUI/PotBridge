## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original polished anime fan-art portrait of Sakurakouji Luna (桜小路ルナ / 樱小路露娜) from Tsuki ni Yorisou Otome no Sahou for a refined desktop browser-extension theme. Input image is a CHARACTER IDENTITY REFERENCE, not an edit target: faithfully preserve Luna's waist-length silver-white hair, braided crown across the top of her head, very pale skin, crimson ruby eyes, and two large black ribbon bows at the sides of her head. Outfit based on the cream Filia academy winter uniform on the LEFT side of reference: fully covered, modest cream tailored dress/blazer, black lapel piping, dark checkered ribbon bow, white lace high collar. One single character, bust-to-waist portrait, calm reserved slightly proud expression, looking gently toward the viewer's left. Hands out of frame. Clean graceful silhouette, flowing hair, understated aristocratic elegance. Fine delicate linework, restrained soft painted shading, silver/ivory/charcoal palette with ruby eyes, warm cream subtle light. Portrait composition with whole head and both ribbon bows fully within frame, no cut-off hair at top or sides. Actual transparent background, no scenery, no lettering, no logo, no border, no other character. This will be placed on the right side of a header at small size: the face must stay crisp and recognizable. Do not render any UI.
